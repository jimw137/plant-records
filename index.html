1. Getting started

Plant Records is your garden notebook. Each plant has its own page with facts, links, notes and two photos.

The app saves by itself. Look at the small message at the right of the toolbar:

  • Unsaved changes… means it's about to save. Wait a second or two.
  • All changes saved · 14:32 means your work is safe.

Tip: before you close the app, check it says All changes saved.

2. Find a plant

  1. Click the search box: Find a plant — start typing…
  2. Type part of the name, the Latin name or a tag.
  3. Click the plant in the list. Or use the ↑ ↓ arrow keys and press Enter.

To look through all your plants as pictures, use Browse. See section 5.

After opening a plant, click ← Back to return to your search list or your Browse results.

3. Change a plant's details

  1. Click any text: the name, the Latin name, a fact, a photo caption or the notes.
  2. Type your changes. A dotted line shows where you're typing.
  3. That's it. It saves by itself.

Grey slanted text means that fact is missing, for example Not sure. Click it and type the real fact.

If you want to save straight away, click Save changes.

Text too big? Click Smaller text. Click again to switch back.

4. Add photos

Each plant has two photo boxes: Whole plant and Close-up. Under each box are two buttons:

📋 Paste⬆ Upload

  • Upload: pick a photo from your computer or phone.
  • Paste: first copy a picture (right-click on it → Copy image), then click Paste.
  • Drag: drag a photo file onto the box.

A new photo replaces the old one in that box. The app shrinks big photos so it stays fast.

Click the caption under a photo to change it.

Lots of your own photos at once: click 📷 Add photos in the toolbar, then 📂 Choose photos… and pick as many as you like (hold Ctrl to pick several, or Ctrl+A for a whole folder).

  1. Next to each photo, type the plant name and click it in the list. Click a photo to see it bigger.
  2. Choose Whole plant or Close-up. Leave it on Skip to ignore that photo.
  3. If the plant isn't in the app yet, click ➕ New plant called … and it will be created for you.
  4. Click Add photos and wait until it says it's finished.

Photos from an iPhone may be HEIC files, which the browser can't read. Set the phone to save as JPG ("Most Compatible"), or convert them first.

5. Browse all your plants

Click Browse to see your plants as cards. Click a card to open that plant.

The spots on each card:

  • All 8 facts filled in
  • Mostly done (5 to 7 facts)
  • A lot to do (0 to 4 facts)
  • Not growing now (the card is faded too)
  • Want to grow
  • ✓ Checked You've checked the facts are right

Show only some plants:

  • The Show: buttons: 🌱 Growing All and so on.
  • The drop-down lists pick a tag, category, flowering season or Draft/Checked.
  • The search box finds plants by name or tag.

After opening a card, click ← Back to return to the same list, with your filters still set.

Tip: to find plants that still need something, use the Category list's No category set option, or look for red and orange spots.

Click Photos to see only pictures. Click one to make it big. Use ‹ › or the arrow keys to move through them.

Press Esc or click Close to go back.

6. Add or delete a plant

Add: click + New plant. A blank page called Untitled plant opens. Click the name and type the real one.

Delete: open the plant, then click Delete this plant and confirm.

⚠ There's no undo button. You can only get a deleted plant back from a backup (section 11). If you might grow it again, set it to Not growing now instead (section 7).

7. Tags, category, colour and status

These buttons sit under the plant name.

  • Category (Perennials, Shrubs, Trees…): pick from the green drop-down list.
  • Growing status: 🌱 Growing ○ Not growing now ☆ Want to grow
  • Draft or Checked: click ☐ Draft — mark as checked once you've checked the facts. It turns green: ✓ Checked
  • Colour: 🎨 Auto (from tags) tints the top of the page. Pick a colour, or No colour.

Tags are short labels like full-sun or summer-flowering. They help you find plants.

  • Add one: type in add a tag then click + Add tag.
  • Remove one: click the × on the tag.

8. Links, notes and research buttons

Links (middle column): click + Add link, paste the web address, then give it a short name.

Notes: the lined paper under the links. Write anything you like.

Research buttons open a new browser tab:

  • 🔍 Search by pictures shows Google pictures of this plant.
  • ▶ Videos shows YouTube videos about it.
  • Plants & Seeds ▾ and the other lists are your saved shop websites. You can add your own at the bottom of each list.
  • 🔎 Identify with Google Lens lets you paste a photo to find out what it is.

9. Import and update many plants

⚠ Make a backup first (section 11).

Import plants adds lots of new plants at once from a text file or pasted text.

  • Each plant starts with its Latin name and common name, then the headings (Description, Site & Soil, Watering and so on).
  • Put a line of ===== between plants.
  • The app checks for plants you already have before adding anything. Read the list carefully.

Update existing fills in missing facts for plants you already have. It only fills blank or Not sure facts. It never changes what you've written.

  • It finds each plant by its exact common or Latin name, so keep names spelled the same as in the app.
  • Any plant it can't find is listed as not matched (skipped). Nothing is changed for those.
  • Text like MISSING — add your own notes counts as written, so it won't be replaced. Type over it yourself.

10. Tidy up your tags

Click ⚙ Manage tags to:

  • Rename a tag to fix a spelling.
  • Merge two tags: rename one to the same name as the other.
  • Delete a tag from every plant.
  • Give a tag a colour. Plants with that tag get that colour at the top.

⚠ These changes affect all your plants at once. Back up first.

11. Backups (important!)

Your plants and photos are stored inside this browser, on this device only. Nothing is kept online. A backup is a copy saved as a file, in case something goes wrong.

⚠ Clearing your browser's history/site data, or uninstalling the browser, can erase your plants. Keep regular backups!

Click Backup and choose:

  • Records only: all the words, tags and links. Small and quick.
  • Records + photos: everything. A bigger file that takes a minute or two.
Good backup habits
  1. Records only at the end of each day you make changes.
  2. Records + photos once a month, and after adding lots of photos.
  3. Always back up before Import, Update existing, Add photos (if it will replace photos), Manage tags or deleting plants.
  4. Keep backups in one folder, for example Plant backups. The date is already in the file name.
  5. Once a month, copy the newest backup to a USB stick or cloud drive.
  6. Keep the last few backups. Delete very old ones.

The Backup window shows when you last made a backup on this device.

To bring plants back from a backup:

  1. Click Backup, then 📂 Choose backup file…
  2. Pick your backup file. The app tells you how many plants are in it, and how many are missing from the app.
  3. Choose one:
    • Add back missing plants is safe. It only puts back plants that aren't in the app now.
    • Replace everything with this backup is only for when the app has gone badly wrong. You have to click it twice.

Use a Records + photos backup if you can, so any lost photos come back too.

12. Troubleshooting

The app stops working or acts strangely
Try these in order. Stop as soon as it's fixed.
  1. Reload the page (press F5). This fixes most small problems. Nothing is lost.
  2. Plants wrong or missing? Use Restore (section 11). Try Add back missing plants first. Use Replace everything only if a lot has gone wrong.
  3. Still broken, or won't open? Ask Claude to fix the app files and upload them to GitHub again. Your plants stay in the browser; if they are lost, use Restore to load your newest backup.
It says "Could not save — try again"
Check you're online, then click Save changes. Don't close the app until it says All changes saved.
My changes disappeared
Each device (and each browser) keeps its own separate copy. Changes on your phone don't appear on your laptop. To move everything across, make a Records + photos backup on one device and restore it on the other with Replace everything.
I deleted a plant by mistake
Click Backup, choose your latest backup file, then click Add back missing plants. See section 11.
A plant is missing from Browse
A filter is probably hiding it. Click All in the Show row, set the drop-down lists back to "Any…" and clear the search box.
I typed on the wrong plant
Always check the big plant name at the top before typing. Then copy your text into the right plant and fix the wrong one.
Paste doesn't put in my photo
Copy the picture itself, not the file or the web address. If your browser asks to allow the clipboard, say yes. Or right-click the photo box and choose Paste. Or use Upload.
My photo looks a bit soft
That's normal. The app shrinks photos so it doesn't get slow.
It says "unavailable here"
This browser is blocking storage, for example a private/incognito window. Open the app normally from its link or the installed app icon.
I can't see the ← Back button
It only appears after you open a plant from Browse or from the search list. It stays for a few hours, then goes until you open another plant that way.
Add photos skipped some of my photos
Those are probably HEIC photos from an iPhone, which the browser can't read. Convert them to JPG, or set the phone camera to Most Compatible so it saves JPGs. Then add them again.
Add photos warns "another photo is going to the same spot"
Two photos are set to the same plant and the same box. Only the last one is kept. Change one to the other box, or to Skip.
I deleted the app from my desktop
Removing the desktop shortcut is fine. But uninstalling the app may also delete its plants, so make a backup first. To reinstall, open the app's link in your browser and click the install icon at the right of the address bar (or the browser menu → Install).
It says "That file is not a Plant Records backup"
You picked the wrong file. Backup files end in .json and their names start with plant-records-backup.
Import added the same plant twice
Delete the extra one. Next time, read the duplicate list before you click to confirm.
Several plants have the same name
That's fine if they're different varieties, like the Japanese anemones. The Latin name tells them apart.
The top of the page is a strange colour
The colour comes from a tag. Change the 🎨 drop-down to No colour or pick a different one.
Everything is too small or too big
Hold Ctrl and press + or −. Press Ctrl + 0 to reset.
